# Dagangan

> Dagangan is an Indonesian tech-enabled wholesale e-commerce platform serving small and medium retailers (SMEs) in tier 3-4 cities and rural areas. Through a hub-and-spoke network of 50+ micro-warehouses, it connects large suppliers to traditional local retailers, giving them affordable mobile access to daily necessities and groceries. Dagangan is backed by 500 Global and recognized as a Rural Digitalization Partner of Indonesia's Ministry of Villages, Disadvantaged Regions, and Transmigration.

Note: Dagangan operates a consumer/retailer mobile shopping application (Android) and a corporate website.
